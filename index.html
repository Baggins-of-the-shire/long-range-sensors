Long Range Sensors

Class

Robotic telescopes on three continents, pointed at things that were already old when the light left them. Some of these came out. The rest are here anyway, with the fault written on the label.

  3. 03 / 10

    Andromeda Galaxy

    M31
    Degraded · Blown core
    Instrument
    Canary Three · Deep-Sky
    Site
    Teide Observatory, Canary Islands
    Filter
    M
    Captured
    2026-09-04 02:06 UTC

    The same galaxy as the banded frame further down the log, caught the same night on a different scope, and this time the dust lanes came through. The core is the problem. It is clipped to flat white with visible steps around it, which is what happens when the exposure that brings out the arms overwhelms the middle. Two companion galaxies are in the frame as well, one above the core and one below.
